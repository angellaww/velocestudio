01 / The Brief

Subverting Monolithic Gravity

Maison Vaneau sought a total redefinition of their autumn/winter tailoring taxonomy. The imperative: bridge the razor-sharp structural rigidity of mid-century brutalist facades with the visceral, liquid tactility of bias-cut drapery.

32 Commercial Validation

Every silhouette produced for the runway debuted at Palais de Tokyo with strict collector caps. 100% of the initial 32 edition-sets sold out within 72 hours of private salon distribution.

02 / Art Direction

The collection's nomenclature, L'Ombre, explores how garments inhabit shifting light. Rather than relying on traditional pads and interfacings, internal canvases were engineered using sculpted horsehair webbing and Italian raw virgin wool.
